# Procurement Policy Excerpt (Sanitized)

**Source type:** authored policy document. ReliabilityTier T2.
**Owner:** finance governance office.
**Version:** 4.2 (last revised Q1 2026).

## Scope

This policy defines the approval workflow for vendor purchases by the organization. It covers all direct vendor spend regardless of department; SaaS subscriptions renewing annually; and professional-services engagements.

## Approval Thresholds

Dollar-bracket approval requirements:

| Bracket | Finance approver | Board escalation |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 9,999 | line manager | no |
| 10,000 to 49,999 | department head | no |
| 50,000 and above | finance VP | required if > 500,000 |

## Finance authority

The finance organization has **sole authority** to approve any purchase whose total contract value meets or exceeds the 50,000 threshold. No other function may authorize such a purchase without finance sign-off.

## Non-standard clauses

Any purchase whose contract contains non-standard indemnity, liability, or IP-assignment clauses MUST receive legal review before execution. This policy does NOT specify whether legal review is a blocking gate or a concurrent advisory function. Interpretation has historically varied by business unit.

## Operational impact

This policy does NOT address purchases affecting operational capacity (e.g., infrastructure SaaS, on-call tooling). Practice in affected business units has been inconsistent. Section reserved for future revision.
