# Customer SLA Contract — Enterprise Tier

## Section 4.1 P0 Incident Acknowledgement

For P0 incidents (severity-1 service-impacting), the Service Provider
agrees to acknowledge the incident within sixty (60) seconds of the
customer's notification through any approved escalation channel.

Acknowledgement constitutes a non-automated response from a named
on-call engineer (no auto-reply emails, no chat-bot ticks). The 60-
second target is contractually binding and breach exposes the Service
Provider to credit obligations per Section 8.

This contract is the active enterprise-tier SLA in effect as of the
contract effective date 2026-01-01; renewal cycle is annual.
