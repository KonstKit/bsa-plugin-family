# Finance PM Interview Transcript (Sanitized)

**Source type:** attestation. ReliabilityTier T4.
**Interviewee role:** finance PM (program manager in the finance organization).
**Date:** current week.
**Disclaimers:** single-stakeholder recall; terms like "always" and "usually" are self-reported.

---

**Interviewer:** Walk me through a typical high-value purchase approval.

**Finance PM:** For anything at 50,000 or above, finance is the approver. The VP signs. Nothing moves without that signature. If legal has concerns they can comment, but finance's call is final — the policy says it plainly.

**Interviewer:** What if legal flags a non-standard indemnity clause?

**Finance PM:** They flag it, they don't block it. We take the flag into account. If the VP still approves, the deal goes through. Legal is advisory on contract terms, not a gate.

**Interviewer:** And operational impact? SaaS renewals that affect on-call tooling?

**Finance PM:** That's the same flow. Dollar bracket drives it. Ops can ask for lead time to transition if the renewal decision changes, but they don't approve or reject.

**Interviewer:** How long does the full flow take?

**Finance PM:** Usually three business days once the request hits the finance inbox. Legal review, if needed, adds maybe two days. Not blocking.
