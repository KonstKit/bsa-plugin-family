# Interview Notes — Product Manager on Triage Pain Points (Source 002)

Origin: recorded interview transcript excerpt, 2026-02-17, PM "Alex" (role: product).

Alex: "The biggest recurring issue is duplicate tickets — the system doesn't flag near-duplicates at intake, so agents close them after the fact. We estimate maybe fifteen percent of all tickets are duplicates."

Alex: "Escalations to engineering are the slowest path. Agents sometimes escalate without attaching the reproduction steps, and engineering sends it back, which adds another half day."

Alex: "Billing escalations work fine because the billing team has their own intake form, so we just tag and forward. No complaints there."

Alex: "We want to keep the four-outcome triage model, but we need better detection of duplicates before an agent claims the ticket."
